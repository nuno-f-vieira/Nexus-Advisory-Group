Last updated: January 2025  ·  Nexus Advisory Group, Lisbon, Portugal

1. Who we are

Nexus Advisory Group ("we", "us", "our") is a strategic advisory firm based in Lisbon, Portugal. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect personal information provided to us through this website or in the course of our advisory services.

For the purposes of applicable data protection law, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Portuguese Law No. 58/2019, Nexus Advisory Group acts as the data controller.

2. Information we collect

We may collect and process the following categories of personal data:

  • Contact information — name, email address, company name, and any details you provide via our enquiry form
  • Professional information — role, organisation, and areas of interest relevant to our services
  • Technical data — IP address, browser type, and general usage data collected via standard server logs or analytics
  • Communications — records of correspondence you initiate with us

3. How we use your information

We process your personal data for the following purposes and on the following legal bases:

  • Responding to enquiries — to reply to messages you send us (legitimate interest / contractual necessity)
  • Service delivery — to provide advisory services you have engaged us for (performance of a contract)
  • Legal compliance — to meet our obligations under applicable law (legal obligation)
  • Business development — to inform you of services that may be relevant to you, where you have not opted out (legitimate interest)

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties.

4. Data retention

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, or as required by law. Enquiry data is typically retained for up to 3 years unless an ongoing advisory relationship is established.

5. Your rights

Under the GDPR, you have the right to: access the personal data we hold about you; request correction of inaccurate data; request erasure in certain circumstances; object to or restrict processing; request data portability; and lodge a complaint with the Portuguese data protection authority (CNPD) at www.cnpd.pt.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at info@nexus-advisorygroup.com.

6. Cookies and tracking

This website does not use tracking or advertising cookies. Basic server logs may capture technical data as described above. No third-party analytics or advertising scripts are loaded.

7. Security

We apply appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data against accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, or unauthorised disclosure.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by an updated date at the top of this document.

9. Contact

For any questions about this Privacy Policy or your personal data, please contact us at: info@nexus-advisorygroup.com
